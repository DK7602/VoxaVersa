VoxaVersa User Manual

Turn your own vocal into another singer, or have a voice sing lyrics you type.

Welcome

VoxaVersa is a vocal plug-in and standalone app. It does two things:

  • Voice Transform: you sing (live or on a recorded track) and VoxaVersa re-sings it as the voice you choose: 46 built-in voices (a male and a female voice in each of 20 genres, plus six specialty voices such as Deep Bass, Whisper and Choir Ensemble).
  • Text to Sing: type lyrics, choose or play a melody, and the selected voice sings them.

It comes in two plug-in versions and an app:

The standalone app is the effect version with its own audio settings (Options → Audio/MIDI Settings): plug in a mic, pick a voice and sing.

Install and update

  1. Close your DAW.
  2. Run the VoxaVersa Setup installer. It installs both plug-ins into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, the standalone app, and the runtime the AI engine needs. It also removes old copies of VoxaVersa from your VST3 folders, so your DAW can't load an outdated one.
  3. Open your DAW and let it rescan plug-ins. VoxaVersa appears under Vocals, VoxaVersa Singer under Instrument.

Adding AI singers

The AI voices are sung by trained AI singers that come as voice packs (.vxpack files, or a zip of them). Open VoxaVersa, click the menu (top right) → Import Voices… and pick the file. A pack with the same name replaces the older one. Your voices, presets and settings are kept when you update VoxaVersa.

If your DAW still lists VoxaVersa in an old category or shows an old version after updating, rescan its plug-ins. In Cubase: Studio → VST Plug-in Manager, rescan (or restart Cubase).

Quick start: transform a vocal track

  1. Insert VoxaVersa on your vocal track (from the Vocals category).
  2. Pick a voice in the Voice Library: click a card. Use Male / Female, Styles, Genres, ★ favourites or search to find one.
  3. Press play. The Input panel shows your vocal, the Output panel the new voice.
  4. Adjust Voice Character if you like (Pitch, Gender, Age, Tone, Style), and Mix to blend in your own voice.
  5. For the finished vocal, export or bounce in your DAW (Export Audio Mixdown, Render in Place or Freeze).
You don't need to press VoxaVersa's own Render button for a track: VoxaVersa works as the track plays. Render is only for audio files you drop onto VoxaVersa itself (see Exporting).

VoxaVersa remembers the voice, its style and every setting with your project, per track.

The window

Header (top)

  • Power: VoxaVersa on/off. It is the same switch as your DAW's bypass. When off, your voice passes through, still in time.
  • Preset name and arrows: browse presets by category, save your own (see Presets).
  • A / B: two settings slots to compare. Right-click to copy one to the other.
  • Undo / Redo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z).
  • Menu (top right): UI size, Quality, Source Voice, the AI Voice Engine (on/off, CPU or GPU, its status), Import Voices, Open / Rescan the voices folder, preset files, and this manual.

Voice Library

Every voice is a card with a portrait. Click to select; double-click to select and close the expanded library. Filter with Male, Female, Styles, Genres, ★ favourites, or search by name, genre, style or description. Right-click a card for more:

  • Add to Favorites.
  • Set as Blend Voice: morph between the selected voice and this one with the Voice Blend control.
  • AI Singer (built-in voices): choose which trained AI singer sings this voice. Automatic uses the standard one. Your choice is kept, even when you import new voices.
  • Style (some voices): the voice's other sounds, see Voice styles.

Input and Output

The Input panel shows what comes in: live input or a voice sample (drag an audio file onto it). The Output panel shows the transformed voice and holds Render (the track at full AI quality, see The AI voice engine), Record and the exports folder.

Voice Character

  • Pitch: higher or deeper in semitones (±24), Fine in cents.
  • Gender: moves the vocal-tract size from male to female.
  • Age: young (smaller, brighter, breathier) to mature (darker, wider vibrato, a little grit).
  • Tone: bright to dark.
  • Style: natural to creative (more vibrato, scoops, grit and saturation; also scales the growl of voices that have one).
  • Intensity: how strongly the voice's character is applied. 60 - 80 % sounds most natural.
  • Mix: your original voice vs. the new one. Output: level.

Specialty voices

  • Deep Bass: a cavernous low bass, an octave below you when the song allows.
  • Angelic Soprano: pure, ethereal head voice.
  • Smoky Alto: low, husky contralto.
  • Whisper: your own voice turned into a breathy whisper (pitch gone, words kept). It is an effect, not an AI singer.
  • Hard Tune Robot: snaps every note at once, the classic hard-tune sound, even with Tune off.
  • Choir Ensemble: a whole section: the lead singer, a singer of the other gender an octave away, and six copies of the two, spread across the stereo field. It uses about twice the AI engine's work. The Doubler's amount and width scale the section.

Accents

With their AI singer on, these voices sing in their style's accent, live, in whatever you sing:

  • Country (male and female): the Southern "ah" in I, my, time, like, and a drawl.
  • Reggae: Jamaican vowels and consonants (tink, dat, de, an' you, fo).
  • Latin: Spanish-accented: a rolled r, jou, gurrl, en for "and".
  • Afrobeats: Nigerian-accented vowels and consonants.

Singer cards

An imported AI singer that doesn't sing any built-in voice appears as a voice of its own, with a portrait and a style title (for example Lyric Tenor or Velvet Mezzo).

The AI voice engine

VoxaVersa has two engines. The DSP engine reshapes your own voice instantly (pitch, formants, tone): great for pitch and gender changes, tuning and harmonies. The AI engine re-sings your performance with a trained singer's voice: this is what makes it sound like a different, real person. Turn it on or off in the menu → Use AI Voice Engine.

Delay and monitoring

The AI voice is about 0.9 seconds late. VoxaVersa tells your DAW, which lines it up automatically, so playback and exports are in time. But if you monitor yourself live through an AI voice, you hear yourself 0.9 s late. Record with the AI engine off (or Quality Low Latency), then switch it on for playback.

Playback vs. export

The AI engine converts in short chunks as the track plays. If your computer can't keep up for a moment, VoxaVersa fills that moment with the DSP voice, so playback never drops out (you'll see a message). Exports, Render in Place and Freeze always wait for every chunk and use the AI engine's best quality.

Render: hear the full quality while you mix

Live playback converts in short chunks, with little time per chunk, so a slower computer uses less context and the voice can sound less like its singer. Render (top of the Output panel) gives you the best quality during playback:

  1. Click Render. It says Play to render.
  2. Play your vocal once, the whole song or just a part. VoxaVersa keeps what it hears.
  3. Press Stop. It renders in the background (Rendering 40%); the live voice plays meanwhile.
  4. When it says Rendered, playback uses the render wherever you played, and the live voice everywhere else.

The render converts each stretch at once, with context on both sides, exactly as an export does. Change the voice or a setting and it renders again by itself (Mix and Output Gain apply without a new render). After you edit or re-record the vocal, click Render → Capture Again; Capture More Parts adds parts you haven't played yet; Turn Off goes back to the live voice. The render is saved with your project (its input is kept in VoxaVersa/Renders) and comes back when you reopen it. Not rendered yet: a choir voice's second section and MIDI Pitch Control (the live voice plays for those).

CPU or GPU, and the status line

The menu shows the engine's status: whether it runs on the CPU or the graphics card, and its real-time load. Under 80 % plays live without gaps. AI Device lets you choose: CPU (recommended) or Graphics card (GPU) (experimental).

Quality

Quality in the menu: Low Latency (about 35 ms, for live monitoring with the DSP engine), Balanced (default) or High Quality (for mixing). Some DAWs (Cubase) only pick up a change of delay, e.g. switching between an AI voice and a DSP-only voice, after you stop and restart playback or switch the plug-in off and on.

Text to Sing

  1. Switch to Text to Sing and type your lyrics.
    • A new line is a new phrase (with a breath).
    • beau-ti-ful splits syllables by hand. _ holds the previous syllable over the next note.
    • [br] inserts a breath or rest.
  2. Choose the Melody Source:
    • Use MIDI: play notes into VoxaVersa; each note sings the next syllable live. Turn on Capture to record what you play, then Generate a polished take from it.
    • Auto Melody: VoxaVersa writes a melody in your Key, Scale and Tempo, shaped by the voice's genre.
    • MIDI File: load a .mid melody.
    • Hum Melody: hum or sing the tune into your mic and VoxaVersa transcribes it.
  3. Press Generate. Preview it, then Render & Drag the WAV into your DAW, or Export MIDI (with the lyrics as MIDI lyric events). In a DAW the take also plays in sync from bar 1.

The voice's register, breathiness, vibrato and phrasing come from the selected voice, and every Voice Character and FX control still applies. Country, Reggae, Latin and Afrobeats voices sing in their style's English.

VoxaVersa Singer and MIDI

VoxaVersa Singer is the instrument version: put it on an instrument track and the MIDI notes on that track sing your lyrics. It is the easiest way to drive Text to Sing with MIDI. To transform a vocal with it, send audio to its sidechain input.

When in doubt, use VoxaVersa Singer on an instrument track: MIDI just works there.

Presets

  • Browse factory presets by category from the preset name in the header; step with the arrows.
  • Save Preset… stores your own; Import Preset… adds a .vxpreset file.
  • A factory preset keeps your session's mode, input/output level, key and scale, MIDI Pitch Control and Text to Sing tempo.
  • Choosing a voice after loading a preset changes only the voice. Reopening the project brings back exactly what you left.

Exporting your vocal

From a track (VoxaVersa as an insert)

Use your DAW: Export Audio Mixdown, Render in Place, Bounce or Freeze. These give the AI voice its best quality. To hear that quality while you mix, use Render.

From an audio file (voice sample)

Drag an audio file onto the Input panel. Press ▶ to hear it through the current voice, then Render to make a WAV and drag it into your DAW. Renders are also saved in Documents\VoxaVersa\Exports.

Text to Sing takes

Render & Drag the take, or Export MIDI.

Recording (standalone app)

Record the transformed output to a WAV with the record button; files go to Documents\VoxaVersa\Recordings.

Tips for natural results

  • Record dry, close and clean. Reverb or background noise on the input confuses pitch tracking.
  • Sing in a comfortable range and leave Pitch Preserve off, so VoxaVersa moves you into the voice's register.
  • Keep Intensity around 60 - 80 %. Push it for character effects.
  • For gender changes, combine the register shift with Gender ±30 - 60 rather than maxing one control.
  • Use High Quality when mixing and Low Latency only when you need to monitor live.
  • Voices with a growl or rasp (Male Blues, Jazz, Male Rap) respond to how you sing: dig in at the start of a phrase or sing out to bring it in. The Style knob scales it.

Troubleshooting and FAQ

Do I need to render when I put VoxaVersa on a track?
No. It works as the track plays. For the finished vocal, export or bounce in your DAW.
I hear myself late while recording.
The AI voice is about 0.9 s late. Record with the AI engine off (menu → Use AI Voice Engine) or Quality Low Latency, then turn it back on for playback.
Sometimes the voice sounds less realistic for a moment during playback.
Your computer didn't finish an AI chunk in time, so VoxaVersa filled in with the DSP voice. Exports are always complete. Check the real-time load in the menu; close other heavy plug-ins, or freeze tracks.
VoxaVersa is in the wrong category, or my DAW loads an old version.
Rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Cubase: Studio → VST Plug-in Manager). The installer removes old copies from your VST3 folders.
The delay is wrong after switching between an AI voice and a non-AI voice (Cubase).
Stop and restart playback, or switch the plug-in off and on in its slot.
A voice's AI singer isn't the one I want.
Right-click the voice → AI Singer and pick another, or Automatic to go back.
An AI voice sounds like the DSP voice, or the status says no model.
Check the menu: Use AI Voice Engine must be on, and its singer pack imported (Import Voices…). Use Rescan Voices after copying packs into the voices folder by hand.
Where is my export?
VoxaVersa's own renders are in Documents\VoxaVersa\Exports (Output panel → exports folder).

AI disclosure

Every WAV VoxaVersa renders or records carries a short note in its metadata saying it is an AI-generated or digitally transformed vocal. AI-voice and Text to Sing output also carries an inaudible watermark, live and in exports, as the EU AI Act (Article 50) asks of synthetic audio. Please keep these notes intact and disclose AI vocals where your distributor or platform requires it. A plain DSP transform of your own voice is not watermarked.
